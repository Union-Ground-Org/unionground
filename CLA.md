This document has two parts:

- **Part A, the Individual Contributor License Agreement**, which every person
  who contributes signs, and
- **Part B, the Entity Contributor License Agreement**, which an employer or
  other organization signs when its people contribute on its behalf.

Thank you for your interest in Union Ground (the "Project"), owned and
maintained by Union Ground LLC (the "Company"). This agreement documents the
rights you grant in your Contributions. It protects you as a contributor and
protects the Company and the Project's users. It does not change your rights
to use your own Contributions for any other purpose.

Why the Company asks for these rights: the Project is published under the GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only), and the Company
also runs an official hosted service and publishes official apps built from
the Project, some parts of which are not public. To use Contributions in those
products, the Company needs the right to license Contributions under other
terms, including proprietary terms.

## Part B: Entity Contributor License Agreement

This part is for a corporation, nonprofit, government body or other
organization (the "Entity") whose employees or members contribute to the
Project as part of their work for the Entity.

The Entity accepts and agrees to sections 1 through 9 of Part A as if each
reference to "You" were a reference to the Entity, for all Contributions
submitted by the people the Entity designates ("Designated Contributors"),
with the following additions:

### B1. Designated Contributors

The Entity gives the Company a list of the people authorized to submit
Contributions on its behalf (names and GitHub usernames), and keeps that list
current. The Entity's Contributions are those submitted by its Designated
Contributors on the Entity's behalf. Each Designated Contributor also signs
Part A.

### B2. Point of contact

The Entity names a point of contact who is responsible for keeping the list
of Designated Contributors current and who receives notices about this
agreement.

### B3. Authority

The person accepting Part B for the Entity represents that they are
authorized to bind the Entity to this agreement.

### B4. Leaving the list

When a person leaves the Entity or is no longer authorized, the Entity
removes them from the list. Contributions submitted before the removal stay
licensed under this agreement.

### How to sign Part B

Email the Company at cla@unionground.org with: the Entity's full legal name
and address; the name, title and email of the person accepting for the
Entity; the point of contact; and the initial list of Designated Contributors
with their GitHub usernames. The Company replies with a copy of this
agreement to countersign, and keeps the signed copy on file.
