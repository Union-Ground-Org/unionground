# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Union Ground serves workers and committees who are **looking to unionize**.
Its hats are Worker, Organizer, Committee, Campaign lead, Advisor, Admin, and
Campaign chair. Every deployment keeps its own members, database, hosting,
and sign-in.

## Product Purpose

Union Ground is the authenticated organizing workspace for forming a union:
building a committee, gathering authorization cards, choosing election or
voluntary recognition, recording employer resistance without calling it law,
bargaining a first contract, and keeping participation honest. Success means
every surfaced action is a sourced next step for a campaign that does not yet
have a contract — never a grievance desk pretending a CBA already exists.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, careful, and empowering. The product speaks in plain language,
protects worker lists, and refuses to present a tactic as universally
effective. It is welcoming to people taking a first organizing step and
serious about retaliation risk.

## Anti-references

- A reskinned member portal for an existing union, with “chapter” and “steward” left in.
- A public page that collects coworker names, card counts, or employer
  allegations.
- Invented win rates, legal conclusions, or “this is an ULP” labels.
- Demo toasts that say nothing was saved for a control the tour invites.

## Design Principles

1. Organizing identities are explicit hats. Navigation never selects Organizer
   or Campaign lead unless the user chose that hat.
2. Worker surfaces use issues, meetings, and organizing rights — not
   grievances, Weingarten, or contract articles — until a first contract
   exists.
3. Private worker activity stays off the public front door.
4. Recognition and election remain separate paths. A card majority is not
   certification.
5. Web and native share one visual language: the website fills the desktop
   plane, and the Expo app owns the iPhone and Android experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. English and Spanish remain complete. Controls used in
break rooms and parking lots stay comfortably touchable.
