# Union Ground public TestFlight beta

Release: 0.3.0 (3)

This beta is the first build of Union Ground's rebuilt native app, with
Union Ground's own palette, mark, organizing hats, and provider boundary.

The beta opens on the provisioned email and password sign-in boundary. Because
a dedicated live provider is not configured, testers should choose **Explore
the PREVIEW demo**. Every PREVIEW person, campaign, employer, meeting, issue,
task, resource, and Guide response is fictional and remains on the test
device. The beta does not connect to another product's provider or copy
another product's session.

## What to test

1. On sign-in, choose **Try the demo**.
2. Open both Home drawers (All tools on the left, Campaign work on the right)
   and return Home to the center.
3. Switch hats from the compact pill: Worker, Organizer, Committee, Campaign
   lead, Advisor, Admin, Campaign chair. Each hat shows its own desk.
4. Use Work in List and Board views, then open and update a sample task.
5. Open the Campaign tab: the path to a first contract, the private preview
   counts, shared issues, and Recognition paths.
6. Ask the Guide one of its suggested questions and verify the answer is
   labeled as guidance from the fictional preview.
7. Open Account; try larger text, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, and the optional
   device lock where available.
8. Send TestFlight feedback with the screen, action, hat, and device model
   involved.

## Review and privacy declarations

- No account is required to review the fictional PREVIEW workspace.
- The build has no advertising, tracking, purchases, paid content, or
  user-generated public content.
- The app declares no non-exempt encryption.
- Preview records and preferences remain in protected local device storage.
- Calendar, camera, microphone, share-sheet, and local-notification features
  act only after the tester requests them; the system calendar editor
  confirms any calendar write. This beta does not register for remote push
  notifications.
- Live authentication, live AI, and a live data provider are unavailable in
  this build.
