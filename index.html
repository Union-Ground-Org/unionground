Union Ground

Union Ground is an organizing workspace for workers and committees who are forming a union.

What the product does

  • Building a committee, gathering authorization cards, and keeping worker lists private
  • Choosing an election or voluntary recognition path, and bargaining a first contract
  • Issues, meetings, and organizing rights for workers, organizers, and committees

The public demonstration uses fictional people, records, and activity.
