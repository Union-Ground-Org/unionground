# Union Ground Trademark Policy

> **Draft pending legal review.** This policy has not yet been reviewed by
> counsel and may change.

The Union Ground code is free software under the GNU Affero General Public
License (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). You can copy, change and share it. The
**name and logo are different**: they tell people that what they are using is
the real Union Ground, run by the people who make it. This policy explains how
you may and may not use them. It is modeled on the Mozilla and Firefox
trademark policies.

## What is official

Only these are "Union Ground":

- the official hosted service run by Union Ground LLC at unionground.org and
  the other domains the LLC lists there;
- the official Union Ground apps published by Union Ground LLC in the Apple
  App Store and Google Play; and
- this source repository and the releases published from it by Union Ground
  LLC.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say where your work comes from.** You may truthfully say that your
  product is "based on Union Ground", "a fork of Union Ground" or "compatible
  with Union Ground", in plain text, as long as your own name comes first and
  nothing suggests the LLC made or endorses it. Modified versions must keep
  the notice "Based on Union Ground (https://unionground.org)" on their about
  or legal screen; that is a license term, not just permission.
- **Talk about Union Ground.** Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials,
  research, classes and discussions about the project.
- **Run unmodified releases.** If you run an unmodified official release for
  your own organization, you may say that it runs Union Ground.
- **Contribute.** Use the name when you refer to this repository, its issues
  and its pull requests.

## What you must not do without permission

- **Ship a modified version under the Union Ground name or logo.** If you
  change the code and publish, host or distribute it, give it your own name,
  logo and app icon. Remove the Union Ground logo and app icon from your
  build.
- **Look like the official service or apps.** Do not use a name, logo,
  domain, app store listing or social media account that could make people
  think they are using the official Union Ground service or apps, or that
  they are dealing with Union Ground LLC. This includes names that are
  confusingly similar, such as misspellings or "Union Ground" plus another
  word.
- **Suggest endorsement or partnership** with Union Ground LLC that does not
  exist.
- **Use the marks in a domain name, company name or product name**, or as
  part of your own logo.
- **Sell merchandise** carrying the marks.

These rules matter because Union Ground handles sensitive information about
workers and the organizations they build. People must be able to tell who is
running the service they trust with that information.

## Asking for permission

If you want to do something this policy does not allow, or you are not sure,
ask first. Email trademarks@unionground.org with what you want to do, where
it will appear, and an example or mock-up. We will reply in writing. Until
you have written permission, assume the answer is no.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the name or logo used in a way that could mislead people, for
example a lookalike site or a copied app, email
trademarks@unionground.org with the link and what you saw.

## Changes

Union Ground LLC may update this policy. The current version is the one in
this repository's default branch.
