# Native app contracts (apps/mobile)

Area contracts for the Expo iPhone and Android app. `docs/contracts/README.md`
lists every area contract.

## Native interaction and depth contract

Owner direction, 2026-09-06:

- Preserve Union Ground's existing theme and exact palette. Use Lovable for design refinement without importing outside colors, providers, identities, or existing-union concepts.
- Home is one full-size moving card above one pinned drawer. Match the sibling animation and 44px facing corners; keep the exposed Home edge available to close. Both side panels must offer useful destinations and actions.
- Guide's composer sits low at rest and immediately above the keyboard when shown. Input screens support downward keyboard dismissal and scrolling to every field.
- Improve existing pages without wholesale redesign, and expand shallow organizing workflows.
- Native integration and acceptance include both physical iPhone and Android phones. Build or browser proof alone is insufficient.

- Owner clarification, 2026-09-06: the native Work screen and widget interactions include List/Board, record drill-down and widget customization, on Union Ground tokens and organizing records.
